Overview

Important Reminder!
Not all setups below will have weapons, characters, or summons that you have, so it's important to test any setups using whatever is available to you. This form of testing is how many Omega setups are created in the first place! Unfortunately, it's unrealistic to list every possible grid permutation and account for every player's inventory.

Seraphic Boost Weapons
As players progress the game, they will be able to replace the weapon that provides a Seraphic Boost with a better weapons. Each upgrade has its own power boosts or activation requirements, so replacing it with a weaker weapon may not yield the same results and could require team or grid adjustments.

Weapons that can be used across all elements.

Celestial Weapons

Limited Event Weapon

  • Celestial weapons are an incredibly potent grid slot due to their combination of powerful offensive and defensive modifiers. Uniquely, the weapons will provide further bonuses to characters whose weapon proficiencies match the Celestial weapon.
  • Adjust the Celestial weapon type based on your party's proficiencies, or choose one for Voltage II synergy if your party's proficiencies are too spread out.
  • At lvl 200, the weapons can be equipped into the additional slots in Party Set Extra teams and are often one of the more powerful options available.
  • Each weapon can also be awakened, giving it the ability to adapt to whatever the team may need. Since awakening requires Crystallized Core ×170, extra care should be taken when choosing an awakening type.
  • See Advanced Grids#Celestial Weapons for more guidance.










Ultima Weapons
  • Ultima weapons at lvl 200 are a staple in many intermediate to advanced grids due to the versatility of its weapon keys and the various effects they provide.
  • The 1st key affects only characters with proficiencies that match the weapon. Commonly used keys include Gauph Key of Strife and Gauph Key of Strength.
  • The 2nd key grants the entire team with a damage cap increase based on the key selected. This key does NOT stack with a Dark Opus Weapon's 2nd key of the same type, so make sure that they are different.
  • The 3rd key is most impactful since with Gauph Key Tria, the weapon now provides a Seraphic-type attack boost to the entire team, thereby replacing Seraphic Weapons. Depending on the team's needs Gauph Key Tessera and Gauph Key Dio may also be useful.
  • Once you have access to 5★ Destroyer Weapons, Ultima Weapons are not as ubiquitous in team setups due to their stronger Seraphic modifiers and other various effects.
  • See Advanced Grids#Ultima Weapons for more guidance.





World Weapons
  • All World weapons possess a damage amplification modifier, however it is limited to a certain damage type and only available to characters with certain weapon proficiencies.
  • As a result, these weapons are very specific to the composition of the team, not just from a character perspective but also the type of damage the team excels at.
  • World weapons can be equipped in the additional slots in Party Set Extra teams.

Grid Templates


It's important to adjust your grid depending on the content you wish to tackle. The grids listed below are serve a general guidelines and should be adapted to what you have available.

Many grid templates will reach 280% aura boost as that is a key break point in optimized grids. This is to activate the powerful bonuses granted by Dark Rapture Zero Pendulums (e.g. Pendulum of Extremity) and Anklets (e.g. Anklet of Oblivion). This threshold is easily met in double-sided setupsSetups with Aura Boosts as both main and support summon, however for single-sided setups, extra consideration is needed.

See #Exalto & Aura Boost for further information.
